01 The fork flow — own your own AdWatch

Fork it. Paste two keys. Done.

You do not rent Fuatilia — you own it. Four steps, nothing to install, no servers to babysit: GitHub's own machines run the radar inside your copy of the repo and deliver the Monday digest to your phone. Free, forever.

Step 1 · 10 seconds

Fork the repo

Open github.com/kjonathan254/fuatilia and press Fork. You now own a complete copy — code, demo data and the weekly workflow included.

Step 2 · 1 click

Switch on Actions

In your fork, open the Actions tab and press the green enable button. That is the whole step — the Monday radar workflow ships inside the repo, ready to go.

Step 3 · 15 minutes

Paste two secrets

Create a Telegram bot with @BotFather, then add FUATILIA_TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and FUATILIA_TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID under Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions. Our tap-by-tap guide walks you through every screen.

Step 4 · 2 minutes

Run it once

Actions → AdWatch → Run workflow. Your first digest lands on your phone before your tea cools. Then every Monday 09:00 Nairobi, automatically, forever.

02 How it works

From A to Z, no jargon.

Eight letters. If you can follow a recipe, you can follow this system.

A

Ads are public

Meta and Google must show the ads they run in Kenya — who is advertising, since when, in how many versions. Anyone can look. We just look carefully, every week.

B

We take notes

Spot an ad card? fuatilia add writes one line in a shared notebook: advertiser, platform, start date, the link. One row, one receipt.

C

The clock starts

Every note remembers its first day. From then on, time does the judging — no opinions, just days counted.

D

90 days = winner candidate

Nobody keeps paying for an ad that loses money for three months. Still alive at 90 days? Something in it works. That is your study material.

E

3 versions = hook testing

Same product, new first line, new photo. They are A/B testing their opening. Watch which version survives — that hook beat the others.

F

30+ ads = team money

1–2 ads is a solo test. 3–29 is someone scaling. 30+ usually means an agency or a funded team. The bands are calibrated for Kenyan budgets.

G

Monday, 09:00 Nairobi

GitHub Actions wakes up, rebuilds the digest and files it — plus Telegram, WhatsApp or webhook delivery when you configure it. Nobody has to remember.

H

You decide

Every output carries the same stamp: signal, not proof. Agencies leave losers on autopilot; grants fund ads that never need profit. Fuatilia classifies. Humans decide.

03 The signals

Three numbers do the reading.

You do not need analytics jargon. The radar only ever counts days and versions.

90+ days

Winner candidate

trigger · ad still live at 90 days

Time is the most honest judge in advertising. A long-lived ad is usually paying for itself. Study its promise, proof, offer, urgency and call to action — copy the structure, never the words.

3+ versions

Hook testing

trigger · same ad, 3 or more active versions

The advertiser is fishing for attention with different openings. The version that survives longest is the hook that worked — worth more than any marketing course.

1–2 · 3–29 · 30+ ads

Scale band

trigger · total active ads per advertiser

Solo testing, then scaling, then team-scale budget. Bands are tuned to Kenyan ad spend, not Silicon Valley ones — 30 active ads here says something very different from 30 in New York.

04 The Monday pipeline

Collect, digest, deliver.

1 · collect lib/collector.js

Pulls the Meta Ad Library API tier (EU + issue ads) into captures.jsonl, de-duplicated. Self-skips without a token.

→
2 · digest lib/digest.js

The Monday Markdown sheet: pulse, winner candidates, hook tests, new entrants, teardown list. Always runs — even on demo data.

→
3 · deliver lib/deliver.js

Sends the digest to Telegram, WhatsApp (Cloud API) or any webhook. Self-skips without secrets.

$ node bin/fuatilia.js digest --out digests  ·  every optional stage switches itself off when unconfigured — a fresh fork runs green on day one.

07 Why Kenyans should care

The intel was already public. Now it is usable.

01The method was free — nobody packaged it for Kenya

The Ad Library shows everything, but it is a firehose of ad cards with no memory. Fuatilia is the bucket: structure, thresholds, a weekly reading habit, and a notebook that remembers what you saw.

02Global "spy tools" skip East Africa

BigSpy, PiPiAds, Foreplay, AdClarity — none ship Kenyan coverage. Enterprise ad intelligence prices out an SMB advertiser in Nairobi. Fuatilia costs nothing, shows its method, and you can read every line of it.

03Small budgets deserve big-market playbooks

If a home-fibre ISP kept one ad alive for four months, a competitor with KSh 5,000 can study that landing page structure before spending a shilling. Winners are teaching for free — Fuatilia makes sure you attend the class.

04It stays legal on purpose

Everything comes from regulator-mandated transparency pages, captured by humans, one ad card at a time. No scraping behind logins, no personal data, no resale of raw platform data. The constraint shaped the product.

Public notice — read before dreaming

  • Signals, not proof. Heuristics classify; humans decide. An old ad can be a funded loser.
  • Spend is not public outside political ads. Anyone selling exact Kenyan spend numbers is guessing.
  • Kenya commercial ads are not in Meta's API — that is why Fuatilia is capture-first: disciplined human capture keeps it inside platform terms.
  • Copy structure, never words or assets. Structure is craft; words belong to whoever wrote them.
  • Demo rows are fictional and labeled example: true. Real captures replace them the moment you run fuatilia add.
