01 The fork flow — own your own AdWatch

Fork it. Paste two keys. Done.

You do not rent Fuatilia — you own it. Four steps, nothing to install, no servers to babysit: GitHub's own machines run the radar inside your copy of the repo and deliver the Monday digest to your phone. Free, and yours.

Step 1 · 10 seconds

Fork the repo

Open github.com/kjonathan254/fuatilia and press Fork. You now own a complete copy — code, demo data and the weekly workflow included.

Step 2 · 1 click

Switch on Actions

In your fork, open the Actions tab and press the green enable button. That is the whole step — the Monday radar workflow ships inside the repo, ready to go.

Step 3 · 15 minutes

Paste two secrets

Create a Telegram bot with @BotFather, then add FUATILIA_TELEGRAM_BOT_TOKEN and FUATILIA_TELEGRAM_CHAT_ID under Settings → Secrets and variables → Actions. Our tap-by-tap guide walks you through every screen.

Step 4 · 2 minutes

Run it once

Actions → AdWatch → Run workflow. Your first digest lands on your phone before your tea cools. Then scheduled every Monday 09:00 Nairobi while your repo stays active — with a heartbeat file proving the last successful run.

02 How it works

From A to Z, no jargon.

Eight letters. If you can follow a recipe, you can follow this system.

A

Ads are public

Meta and Google must show the ads they run in Kenya — who is advertising, since when, in how many versions. Anyone can look. We just look carefully, every week.

B

We take notes

Spot an ad card? fuatilia add writes one line in a shared notebook: advertiser, platform, start date, the link. One row, one receipt.

C

The clock starts

Every note remembers its first day. From then on, time does the judging — no opinions, just days counted.

D

90 days = still standing

An ad alive at 90 days is a persistence signal — profit, brand reach, retargeting, grants or autopilot. Worth studying either way. That is your starting material, and you supply the verdict.

E

3+ variants = experimenting

Same product, new cut, new language, new placement. They are iterating something — count the concepts, not just the variants, and you get creative intelligence instead of ad arithmetic.

F

30+ active ads = wide footprint

1–2 active ads is light activity. 3–29 is consistent. 30+ is a wide footprint. The bands read activity, never budgets — spend is not public.

G

Monday, 09:00 Nairobi

GitHub Actions wakes up, rebuilds the digest and files it — plus Telegram, WhatsApp or webhook delivery when you configure it. Nobody has to remember.

H

You decide

Every output carries the same stamp: signal, not proof. Agencies leave losers on autopilot; grants fund ads that never need profit. Fuatilia classifies. Humans decide.

03 The signals

Three numbers do the reading.

You do not need analytics jargon. The radar only ever counts days and versions.

90+ days

Long-running creative

trigger · ad still live at 90 days

A persistence signal, not a performance verdict: brand campaigns, retargeting, grants and autopilot all keep ads alive. Study the survivors' promise, proof, offer, urgency and CTA — copy the structure, never the words — then judge why it survived.

3+ variants

Creative experimentation

trigger · same ad concept, 3 or more active variants

The advertiser is iterating something — different openings, or just aspect ratios, languages and placements. Track concepts, not just counts; the concept that survives longest is the lesson.

1–2 · 3–29 · 30+ ads

Activity band

trigger · active_ad_count per advertiser — never the variant count

Light, consistent, wide-footprint — activity bands, not budget estimates. Every band prints its basis (reported active ads or captured cards). Spend is not public; anyone selling exact Kenyan spend numbers is guessing.

04 The Monday pipeline

Collect, digest, deliver.

1 · collect lib/collector.js

Pulls the Meta Ad Library API tier (EU + issue ads) into captures.jsonl, de-duplicated. Self-skips without a token.

→
2 · digest lib/digest.js

The Monday Markdown sheet: pulse, long-running creatives, experimenters, new entrants, teardown list. Always runs — demo data is labelled loudly, never passed off as live.

→
3 · deliver lib/deliver.js

Sends the digest to Telegram, WhatsApp (Cloud API) or any webhook. Self-skips without secrets.

$ node bin/fuatilia.js digest --out digests  ·  every optional stage switches itself off when unconfigured — a fresh fork runs green on day one.

08 Why Kenyans should care

The intel was already public. Now it is usable.

01The method was free — nobody packaged it for Kenya

The Ad Library shows everything, but it is a firehose of ad cards with no memory. Fuatilia is the bucket: structure, thresholds, a weekly reading habit, and a notebook that remembers what you saw.

02Global tools are built for other markets

The global ad-intelligence platforms are built around markets with API access and enterprise budgets — neither fits most Kenyan SMBs. Fuatilia is designed around Kenya: local categories, local budgets, local prices. It costs nothing, shows its method, and you can read every line of it.

03Small budgets deserve big-market playbooks

If a home-fibre ISP kept one ad alive for four months, a competitor with KSh 5,000 can study that landing page structure before spending a shilling. Long-running ads teach for free — Fuatilia makes sure you attend the class, and tells you persistence is not proof of profit.

04It stays legal on purpose

Everything comes from publicly available transparency pages, captured by humans, one ad card at a time. No scraping behind logins, no personal data, no resale of raw platform data. Public availability is a fact about access — not a claim about Kenyan law. The constraint shaped the product.

Public notice — read before dreaming

  • Signals, not proof. Heuristics classify; humans decide. A long-running ad can be a funded loser; variants can be formats, not new hooks.
  • Coverage is partial by design. The coverage monitor counts only what was actually captured. Absence here is not absence in the market.
  • Spend is not public outside political ads. Anyone selling exact Kenyan spend numbers is guessing.
  • Kenya commercial ads are not in Meta's API — that is why Fuatilia is capture-first: disciplined human capture keeps it inside platform terms.
  • Copy structure, never words or assets. Structure is craft; words belong to whoever wrote them.
  • Demo rows are fictional and live in data/demo.jsonl, isolated from real captures — badged in the viewer, excluded from coverage counts, and loudly labelled in any demo digest.
